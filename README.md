## About Us

**Josna Technologies Ltd** is a modern software development and technology consulting company based in Dhaka, Bangladesh. We help startups, entrepreneurs, and growing businesses transform ideas into secure, scalable, and thoughtfully designed digital products.

We combine strong engineering, clear communication, and practical business understanding to deliver software that creates lasting value.
